Commercial approach

How I try to work

A few principles I've picked up over the years — some learned the hard way, all shaped by real situations.

Principle 01

Understand the business before pitching

I try to spend as much time as possible understanding what a customer is actually trying to achieve before talking about solutions. The conversations tend to go much better that way.

Principle 02

Keep the pipeline honest

A clean, well-qualified pipeline is more valuable than a large, inflated one. The Prospect Qualification Forum I built at Kadant came from a simple belief: it's better to know the truth early than to be surprised later.

Principle 03

Bring the right people in early

The best commercial outcomes I've been part of involved aligning R&D, engineering, and supply chain well before a proposal was written. It takes more effort upfront, but it almost always leads to a better outcome.

Principle 04

Let data do the convincing

Whether it was outlet-level research for Parle Agro or account prioritisation at Kadant — I've found that decisions backed by data are easier to defend and more likely to stick.

Principle 05

Think about the next chapter, not just the renewal

I try to be thinking about where an account could go next — new categories, adjacent geographies, cross-sell opportunities — well before the current contract becomes the focus of conversation.

Principle 06

Take ownership of the outcome

P&L accountability has taught me to stay close to the numbers — to forecast carefully and flag problems early rather than hoping things work out. It's a habit I've come to value a lot.
