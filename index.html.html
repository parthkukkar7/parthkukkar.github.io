Commercial approach

How I work

A playbook built through real execution — not MBA theory.

Principle 01

Start with the customer's growth agenda

Every conversation begins by understanding what the business is trying to achieve — not what I'm trying to sell. Commercial solutions get mapped to P&L outcomes, making every proposal defensible at board level.

Principle 02

Engineer pipelines, don't chase deals

The Prospect Qualification Forum I built at Kadant transformed reactive selling into a disciplined system. Pipeline health is a leading indicator — I manage it with the same rigour as a revenue number.

Principle 03

Align internally before selling externally

My highest-value deals have all involved aligning R&D, engineering, supply chain, and leadership before a proposal reaches the client. The internal sell is often harder — and more important — than the external one.

Principle 04

Use data to defend commercial positions

Whether it's 450-outlet market intelligence for Parle Agro or Pareto-driven prioritisation at Kadant — I treat data as negotiation currency, not decoration.

Principle 05

Expand before you renew

Retention is table stakes. My account plans always identify the next growth vector — adjacent categories, cross-sell, geographic expansion — 6 to 12 months before the renewal conversation.

Principle 06

Own the number like a business owner

P&L accountability at Kadant and pipeline ownership at Tetra Pak trained me to forecast, track, and course-correct before it's too late. I think like an owner — not a quota carrier.
